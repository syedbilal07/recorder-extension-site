Record

  • Record a tab, a window or your whole screen, with your camera, microphone and tab sound
  • Start from the icon or Ctrl+Shift+Y; pause with Ctrl+Shift+U
  • A custom cursor for tab recordings, and a floating camera preview that stays out of the video

Edit

  • Automatic zooms on your clicks, editable on the timeline
  • Click effects, keyboard shortcuts on screen, drag trails
  • Cut, speed (0.25×–4×), copy and paste clips, with undo
  • Backgrounds, padding, rounded corners, shadow and six aspect ratios
  • A camera bubble with shape, border and shadow that can shrink while zoomed in

Export

  • MP4, WebM and GIF
  • Low, Standard (1080p), HD (1440p) and Max (full resolution)
