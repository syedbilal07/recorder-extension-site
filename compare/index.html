Reframe and the alternatives

Every tool here is good at something. These pages say plainly what each does best, where your videos are stored, and when you’d pick it over Reframe. Checked 29 October 2026; prices change, so follow the links before you decide.

Comparisons

  • Reframe vs Loom

    Loom (by Atlassian) is a cloud video messaging tool: you record, and it gives you a link to share.

  • Reframe vs Screen Studio

    Screen Studio is a macOS app for polished screen recordings with automatic zoom.

  • Reframe vs Screencastify

    Screencastify is a Chrome screen recorder popular in schools, with cloud storage and sharing.

  • Reframe vs OBS Studio

    OBS Studio is free, open-source software for recording and live streaming on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Reframe
