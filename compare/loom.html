Reframe vs Loom

Loom is built around sharing: record, and send a link that your team can watch and comment on. Reframe is built around the video itself: zooms on every click, a real editor, and files that stay on your computer.

Loom (by Atlassian) is a cloud video messaging tool: you record, and it gives you a link to share. Checked 29 October 2026.

Which should you choose?

Choose Loom if…

  • You want to share a link in seconds and see who watched it.
  • Your team comments on videos together in one shared library.
  • You record on a phone as well as a computer.

Choose Reframe if…

  • You want polished videos with zooms on every click, without editing by hand.
  • Recordings must stay on your computer, for example internal tools or customer data.
  • You don’t want an account, a recording-length limit or a per-user subscription.
  • You need an MP4, WebM or GIF file to post or attach anywhere.

Price

Loom has a free plan and paid plans billed per user. Most comparisons in 2026 report the free plan as limited to 25 videos per person and 5 minutes per recording. Check loom.com for current prices and limits.

Reframe is free: recording, editing and exporting, with no account. See the roadmap for what’s planned.
