Reframe vs OBS Studio

OBS Studio is the go-to free tool for streamers and anyone who wants full control over scenes and sources. Reframe is for the opposite case: you want a clear, zoomed-in screen recording in a few minutes, without setting anything up.

OBS Studio is free, open-source software for recording and live streaming on Windows, macOS and Linux. Checked 29 October 2026.

Which should you choose?

Choose OBS Studio if…

  • You live stream, or record games and multi-source setups with scenes and overlays.
  • You want open-source software with plugins for nearly anything.

Choose Reframe if…

  • You want a finished, zoomed-in tutorial or demo without a separate video editor.
  • You’d rather not configure scenes, sources and encoder settings.
  • You mostly record things in the browser.

Price

OBS Studio is free and open source. Reframe is free too.

Reframe is free: recording, editing and exporting, with no account. See the roadmap for what’s planned.
