Reframe vs Screen Studio

Screen Studio made automatic click zoom popular, and it’s a polished Mac app. Reframe brings the same idea to the browser: free, on any computer that runs Chrome, with nothing to install beyond an extension.

Screen Studio is a macOS app for polished screen recordings with automatic zoom. Checked 29 October 2026.

At a glance

FeatureReframeScreen Studio
What it isChrome extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave) on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOSDesktop app for macOS only
Where videos goOn your computer; nothing is uploadedOn your Mac
Automatic zoom on clicksYes: a zoom on every click in tab recordings, editable; add your own anywhereYes, editable on the timeline
RecordsA browser tab, a window or the whole screenAny app, window or the whole screen on your Mac
PriceFreePaid subscription, monthly or yearly

Which should you choose?

Choose Screen Studio if…

  • You’re on a Mac and record desktop apps as often as web apps: its click zoom works everywhere on the Mac.
  • You want a dedicated desktop app with its own deep set of editing options.

Choose Reframe if…

  • You use Windows, Linux or a Chromebook, or a team with mixed computers.
  • You mostly record web apps and sites, where Reframe’s zooms are automatic.
  • You want it free, with no licence to manage.

Price

At the time of checking, Screen Studio was sold as a subscription, about $29 a month or $9 a month billed yearly, with all features on both plans. Earlier one-time licences are reported as no longer sold. Check screen.studio for current prices.

Reframe is free: recording, editing and exporting, with no account. See the roadmap for what’s planned.
