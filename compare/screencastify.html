Reframe vs Screencastify

Both are Chrome extensions. Screencastify is designed around sharing and classrooms; Reframe is designed around the video: automatic zooms, cursor and click effects, and exports that stay on your computer.

Screencastify is a Chrome screen recorder popular in schools, with cloud storage and sharing. Checked 29 October 2026.

Which should you choose?

Choose Screencastify if…

  • You’re a teacher or school using classroom features and assignments.
  • You want videos stored in an account and shared with a link.

Choose Reframe if…

  • You want automatic zooms, styled cursors and click effects without editing by hand.
  • Videos should stay on your computer, with no account.
  • You want exports with no recording limits and no watermark, for free.

Price

Screencastify has a free plan and paid plans; comparisons in 2026 list paid plans from about $7 per month billed yearly. Reports of the free plan’s limits differ, so check screencastify.com for current details.

Reframe is free: recording, editing and exporting, with no account. See the roadmap for what’s planned.
