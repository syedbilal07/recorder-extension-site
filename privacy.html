Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: everything the extension records or creates stays on your computer. It sends nothing to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What the extension stores, and where

All of the following is stored only in your browser's storage for this extension, on your device:

  • Recordings: the video of the tab, window or screen you chose to record, and, if you turned them on, your camera and microphone as separate tracks.
  • Interaction data for automatic zoom and click effects, captured only in the tab you are recording and only while recording: pointer position, clicks and drags, scrolling, the page size, and keyboard shortcuts (such as Ctrl+C or Enter). Typed text is never captured, and nothing at all is captured from password, one-time-code or payment-card fields.
  • Your edits: cuts, zooms, background, layout and other settings for each recording, plus any background image you upload.
  • Exported videos, until you save them elsewhere or export again.
  • Preferences: your last choice of recording source, camera, microphone and theme.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests with your data. When you click Download, the video is saved to the place you choose on your computer. If you share that file, that's your choice and outside the extension.

Permissions, and why they are needed

PermissionUsed for
activeTab, scriptingShowing the recorder on the page you are on when you click the icon or press the shortcut, and capturing interactions in the tab you record. Nothing runs on pages you haven't chosen.
tabCaptureRecording the tab you chose.
offscreenKeeping a tab recording running in the background.
storage, unlimitedStorageSaving recordings, edits and preferences on your device; recordings can be large.
contextMenusA "My recordings" item when you right-click the toolbar icon.
Access to a site, or to all sites (optional, asked for)The first time you record a tab on a website, Chrome asks whether Reframe may access that site; you can say no, and turn the question off in the recorder's options. You can also allow all sites at once. While you record, this lets the recorder keep noting clicks and shortcuts in the recorded tab when it opens another page. Nothing else is read, and nothing leaves your device.
Camera and microphone (asked by Chrome)Only if you choose a camera or microphone, and only while recording.

Deleting your data

Delete any recording from the extension's Recordings page; its video, edits and exports are removed. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.

This website

This website has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address to deliver the pages (see GitHub’s privacy statement). The free tools (video to GIF, video compressor, screenshot beautifier) work entirely in your browser: the files you choose are never uploaded. If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Any change that would send data off your device would be described here first and would require your consent.
