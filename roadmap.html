Roadmap

Where Reframe is heading

What we’re building next and the ideas we’re weighing up. Your feedback decides the order:tell us what you need.

Next

What we’re working on now.

  • Chrome Web Store release

    Reframe 1.0, free for everyone.

  • Reframe Pro

    An optional one-time purchase, with no subscription.

  • Microsoft Edge Add-ons

    A listing in Edge’s own store. Edge can already install Reframe from the Chrome Web Store.

Considering

Ideas we’d like your feedback on. No dates yet.

  • Text and arrows

    Add labels, callouts and arrows on top of the video in the editor.

  • Blur sensitive areas

    Hide emails, names or numbers in part of the screen.

  • Camera background blur

    Soften what’s behind you in the camera bubble.

  • Share links

    An optional link to share a video, for people who want it. Saving files on your computer stays the default.

  • Edit your own videos

    Bring in a video you recorded elsewhere and add zooms and backgrounds.

Shipped in 1.0

Ready for the first release.

  • Automatic zoom

    A zoom on every click in tab recordings, editable on the timeline.

  • Cursor and click effects

    Custom cursor, five click effects, ring while held, drag trail.

  • Keyboard shortcuts on screen

    Shown as you press them; typed text is never recorded.

  • Camera and microphone

    A styled camera bubble that can shrink while zoomed in.

  • Editor

    Cut, speed, copy and paste, backgrounds, aspect ratios, undo.

  • Exports

    MP4, WebM and GIF, from 720p up to full resolution.

  • Recovery

    Interrupted recordings are kept and repaired automatically.

See what changed in each version in the changelog.
