Free tools

Free tools that never upload your files

Small, fast tools for the jobs around a screen recording. They run entirely in your browser: no sign-up, no watermark, and your files stay on your computer.

All tools

  • Video to GIF

    Turn a clip into a looping GIF for docs, chats and emails. Choose the part you want, the size and the smoothness. It all happens in your browser.

  • Video compressor

    Shrink a video so it fits in an email, a ticket or a chat. Pick a quality and a maximum size; the video is re-encoded on your own computer.

  • Screenshot beautifier

    Make a plain screenshot ready for a post, a slide or your docs: add a background, padding, rounded corners, a soft shadow and a window frame.

Need to record your screen first? Reframe records a tab, window or screen and zooms in where you click.
