Screenshot beautifier

Make a plain screenshot ready for a post, a slide or your docs: add a background, padding, rounded corners, a soft shadow and a window frame.

  • Free
  • No sign-up

Background

How to use the screenshot beautifier

  1. Choose a screenshot, drop it on the box, or paste it with Ctrl+V.
  2. Pick a background, padding, corners, shadow, frame and shape.
  3. Download the PNG, or copy it and paste it straight into your post.

Questions

Is my screenshot uploaded?

No. Everything is drawn in your browser. The image never leaves your computer.

What size is the result?

Your screenshot keeps its full resolution; the padding and frame are added around it. The final size is shown under the preview.

Can I get a transparent background?

Yes: choose Transparent. The PNG keeps the shadow and rounded corners with nothing behind them.

Recording your screen?

Reframe is our free Chrome extension: it records a tab, window or screen and zooms in wherever you click, then exports MP4, WebM or GIF. Like this tool, it keeps everything on your computer.
