Use cases

A screen recorder for every “let me show you”

Whenever showing is quicker than explaining, Reframe makes the video easy to follow, with zooms on every click and nothing uploaded.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

By task

  • Bug reports

    A two-line ticket starts a long thread of questions. A short recording answers them before anyone asks: what you clicked, what you pressed and what went wrong, with each step zoomed in so it can be read.

    • QA
    • Developers
    • Support

  • Product demos

    A good demo guides the eye. Reframe follows your clicks with smooth zooms, so viewers always know where to look, and frames it all on a background that fits your brand.

    • Sales
    • Marketing
    • Founders

  • Tutorials

    In a tutorial, the hardest part for viewers is seeing where you clicked. Reframe zooms in on each step and shows your shortcuts, so they can follow along at full speed.

    • Education
    • Documentation
    • Creators

  • Customer support

    “Click the third icon from the left” rarely works. A 30-second recording that zooms in on the right button does, and you can reuse it for the next customer who asks.

    • Support
    • Success
    • Onboarding

  • Team updates

    Show what you built, what changed or what you need, in a two-minute video your team can watch when it suits them. Reframe keeps it easy to follow with zooms on everything you click.

    • Remote teams
    • Product
    • Engineering

  • Social clips

    Feeds are vertical; screens aren’t. Reframe zooms in on what you click, so a wide recording still reads well as a 9:16 or 1:1 clip.

    • Marketing
    • Creators
    • Launches
