Use cases / Product demos

Product demos that look edited, without the editing

A good demo guides the eye. Reframe follows your clicks with smooth zooms, so viewers always know where to look, and frames it all on a background that fits your brand.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web StoreHow to do it

Why Reframe for product demos

  • Zooms that follow you

    Every click becomes a smooth zoom. Retune how close each one gets, move it, delete it or add your own.

  • A cursor that stands out

    Pick a cursor colour and size before you record, and a click effect in the editor. Viewers never lose the pointer.

  • Your face, if you want it

    Add your camera as a circle or rounded square with a border and shadow. Drag it anywhere, and have it shrink while the video is zoomed in.

  • Sharp, on-brand exports

    Choose a wallpaper, gradient, colour or your own image, round the corners, and export up to your screen’s full resolution.

How to record product demos with Reframe

  1. Open your product in a Chrome tab and click the Reframe icon.
  2. Choose This tab, turn on your camera and microphone if you want them, and pick a cursor.
  3. Press Record and walk through the product as you would on a call.
  4. In the editor, check the zooms, cut any stumbles and choose a background and aspect ratio.
  5. Export as MP4 and share it on your site, in a sales email or in your launch post.

Tips

  • Rehearse once on the practice page to get a feel for how zooms follow clicks.
  • Slow down slightly before each important click: zooms look best when the pointer settles.
  • Use HD or Max quality for videos on your website, and 16:9 for most players.

Questions

Can I change a zoom after recording?

Yes. Every zoom is on the timeline. Drag it, change its scale or focus point, delete it, or press Z to add one at the playhead.

Is there a watermark?

No. Exports carry no watermark.

Related use cases

  • Social clips

  • Tutorials
