Use cases / Social clips

Vertical and square clips for social posts

Feeds are vertical; screens aren’t. Reframe zooms in on what you click, so a wide recording still reads well as a 9:16 or 1:1 clip.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web StoreHow to do it

Why Reframe for social clips

  • Every aspect ratio

    Export 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4 or 2:1 from the same recording. Switch any time in the editor.

  • Zooms keep it readable

    Close-ups on each click mean small details still read in a narrow frame.

  • Backgrounds that pop

    Wallpapers, gradients, colours or your own image, with blur, padding, rounded corners and shadow.

  • GIFs too

    Export clips of up to 30 seconds as GIFs for places that don’t play video.

How to record social clips with Reframe

  1. Record your product or feature in a Chrome tab as usual.
  2. In the editor’s Look tab, set the aspect ratio to 9:16 or 1:1.
  3. Pick a background and check the zooms frame what matters.
  4. Cut it down to the best 15–60 seconds.
  5. Export as MP4 and post it.

Tips

  • Zoom in closer (Scale) for vertical clips: there’s less width to work with.
  • Hook viewers in the first two seconds by starting on the most interesting click.
  • Keep the camera off or small in vertical clips so the product stays the focus.

Questions

Do I need to record in a vertical window?

No. Record as usual; the editor places your recording inside the vertical frame and the zooms keep the action visible.

Which formats can I export?

MP4 (H.264), WebM and, for clips up to 30 seconds, GIF.

Related use cases

  • Product demos

  • Tutorials
