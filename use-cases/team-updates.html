Use cases / Team updates

Async updates that replace a meeting

Show what you built, what changed or what you need, in a two-minute video your team can watch when it suits them. Reframe keeps it easy to follow with zooms on everything you click.

Why Reframe for team updates

  • You, on camera

    A small camera bubble keeps it personal. Choose its shape, border and position, or hide it.

  • Easy to skim

    Zooms point at exactly what you’re talking about, so nobody needs to hunt for it on screen.

  • Short by design

    Cut tangents and speed up the slow parts. Paste a clip elsewhere if you want to show something twice.

  • Works anywhere you share files

    Export an MP4 and drop it in Slack, Teams, email or your project tool.

How to record team updates with Reframe

  1. Open what you want to show in a Chrome tab.
  2. Click the Reframe icon, turn on your camera and microphone, and press Record.
  3. Walk through your update. Pause any time with Ctrl+Shift+U.
  4. Trim the start and end and check the camera placement.
  5. Export and share the file with your team.

Tips

  • Lead with the one thing you need from your team, then show the details.
  • Turn on Shrink while zoomed in (Camera tab) so your camera never covers a zoomed-in detail.
  • Low quality is plenty for quick updates and keeps uploads fast.

Questions

Can I record more than one tab?

Record a whole window or your screen to move between tabs and apps freely. Automatic zooms work for single-tab recordings; elsewhere add them with Z.

Is there a time limit?

No. Recordings are limited only by the free space on your computer.
